About Ujjwal Cosmetics

Hi, I am Ujjwal Prajapati, and this shop is my dream. I started it in 2026 with my father's support, right here in Chandupura, because I wanted our neighbourhood to have a beauty shop it can trust.

I remember how hard it was to find the right face wash or sunscreen without someone to guide you. So when you walk in, I will ask about your skin and hair, and tell you honestly what suits you, even if it is the cheaper option.

We are a new shop at A28/87 Chandupura Baluabir, and every customer who trusts us means a lot. Thank you for being a part of our journey.

Ujjwal Prajapati, Owner
